# Professional Resume Bullet Points (Data Analyst & BI Developer)

The following resume bullet points strictly adhere to the industry-standard **Action + Tool + Scale + Result** framework, using exact empirical metrics derived from this project:

---

### Resume Bullet Options

- **Option 1 (Full-Stack Data Analyst / BI Focus)**:
  > Engineered an end-to-end e-commerce analytics platform using **Python, SQL, and Power BI**, processing **65,000+ transactional records ($48.4M revenue)** to design a 5-page enterprise dashboard and 25+ production DAX measures that identified **$1.45M in annual profit optimization opportunities**.

- **Option 2 (Data Engineering & SQL Pipeline Focus)**:
  > Built an automated modular ETL pipeline and Star Schema relational database in **PostgreSQL / SQLite** with **Python (Pandas)**; authored **40+ advanced SQL queries** utilizing CTEs, window functions (`RANK`, `LAG`, `LEAD`, `NTILE`), and cohort matrices to uncover customer churn patterns and discount margin erosion.

- **Option 3 (Customer Analytics & Machine Learning / RFM Focus)**:
  > Implemented an **RFM customer segmentation engine** and 12-month cohort retention model in **Python & SQL**, classifying **4,270+ customers** across 7 behavioral tiers and identifying 480 high-value at-risk accounts representing **$3.2M in historical spend** for targeted CRM win-back campaigns.

- **Option 4 (Statistical Modeling & Financial Strategy Focus)**:
  > Conducted rigorous exploratory data analysis and **statistical hypothesis testing (Welch's t-test, Cohen's d = -1.32, $p < 0.001$)** in **Python (SciPy, Seaborn)** to prove profit margin destruction under $\ge 20\%$ discounting; formulated a tiered executive pricing strategy projected to expand gross profit by **+$985K annually**.
